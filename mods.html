There Can Be Only One: The Knox Gathering

July 1993. Jacob Kell weaponized the Knox plague to isolate North America and force The Gathering early. You start as an ordinary mortal survivor—no starter superpowers—until First Death ignites the Quickening.

Open on Steam Workshop

Core Rules & Canon

  • True Blind-Start: Hidden HUD, silent Buzz, and inert hotkeys until First Death (<= 12% HP trauma or terminal Knox infection >= 85%).
  • Temporary vs. Permanent Death: Critical trauma triggers 15–45 in-game minutes of unconscious Quickening recovery; lose your head in a duel or let 3+ zombies feed on your head/neck and it’s permanent.
  • July 1993 Roster: Kurgan & Ramírez exist only as rare relic weapons; meet allies (Duncan, Connor, Amanda, Methos, Richie) and hunt 6 villains before Jacob Kell.
  • Holy Ground: Churches and cemeteries block duels and Quickening powers.

4-Tier Powers & Controls

  • Tier 1 (First Death): The Buzz 60-tile sense, wound regeneration, +30 carry weight, permanent Knox Virus immunity.
  • Tier 2 Z: Highlander Quickstep kinetic dash & zombie stagger (1 Quickening or Blade Lv. 4).
  • Tier 3 C: Edge of Eternity 12s lightning blade imbuement, +220% damage, +45% decapitation (3 Quickenings or Blade Lv. 7).
  • Tier 4 X: The Gathering 360° lightning shockwave (5 Quickenings or Blade Lv. 9).
  • Dev / Testing F9: Toggle [DEV] Immortal Debug anytime without -debug.

Sanctuary & Watchers

  • MacLeod’s Antique Store (X: 12640, Y: 1840): Louisville ally sanctuary guided by The Buzz at 5+ Quickenings; awards store key, Antique Forged Katana, +2000 Long Blade XP, and full healing.
  • 3 Watcher Listening Posts: Louisville (12580, 1650), Muldraugh (10630, 10180), and West Point (11920, 6860).
  • Endgame Victory: Defeat Jacob Kell to claim The Prize with optional victory screen (click OK to keep playing).

Divine Cheats & Vehicle Suite

Standalone sandbox debug, player god-tools, and full vehicle workshop suite built cleanly for both Build 41 and Build 42 (using native B42 CharacterStat APIs—no -debug launch flags needed).

Open on Steam Workshop

Vehicle Workshop

  • Full Vehicle Repair: Restores engine, body, doors, windows, tires, battery, gas tank, locks, armor plates, roof racks, and bullbars to 100%.
  • Instant Hotwire, Keys & Refuel: Start any ride immediately or spawn a matching ignition key.
  • Vehicle God Mode: Keeps engine and parts invulnerable to zombie hits and collisions.
  • All-Pack Spawner: Paginated spawner scanning Vanilla, Filibuster Rhymes, Autotsar (ATA), KI5, and custom vehicle packs.

Player & Progression

  • God Mode & Full Heal: Invulnerability, grab break, and instant Knox Virus / wound purge.
  • Infinite Carry Weight: Boosts capacity to 10,000 and cleanly restores base weight when toggled off.
  • Ghost Mode & Instant Actions: Zombies ignore you; 1-tick crafting, building, reading, and transfers.
  • Death Aura: Vaporizes zombies within 6 tiles.
  • Max Skills & Learn All Recipes: Level 10 perks + full B42 CraftRecipes unlock.

Kits, World & Travel

  • 5 Curated Supply Kits: Survivalist Heavy Tools, Trauma Medical, Firearms Armory (matching mags + ammo), Mechanic & Builder, Farming & Camping.
  • World Tools: Spawn 1 test zombie, mini horde (10), or clear zombies within 35 tiles.
  • Quick Travel: Safe landmark teleport across Muldraugh, Rosewood, Riverside, West Point, March Ridge, Central Louisville, and the Military Checkpoint.

An American in Camelot

Keep the entire RimWorld planet locked in the Medieval era while your own colony researches and builds all the way to Ultra and Archotech—with one secretive Shattered Empire outpost watching from afar.

Tech Asymmetry

  • Zero Player Tech Caps: Your faction advances freely from Neolithic and Medieval into Industrial, Spacer, and Ultra tech.
  • Medieval NPC Cap: All NPC factions, pawn gear, and non-Empire world loot stay grounded at Medieval tech.

Shattered Empire Rules

  • Lone Outpost: Restricts the Shattered Empire (Royalty) to a single settlement on the whole planet.
  • Player-Only Diplomacy: Permanently neutral with all NPC factions; trades and sends Royal Tribute Collectors exclusively to you.

Compatibility

  • Non-Destructive Harmony: Never deletes XML Defs at load time.
  • Works With: Standalone vanilla + all DLCs, or alongside World Tech Level, Medieval Overhaul, and Vanilla Factions Expanded – Medieval 2.
