There Can Be Only One: The Knox Gathering

July 1993 Highlander survival RPG & Louisville endgame for Project Zomboid. Jacob Kell unleashed the Knox plague to force The Gathering early.

Every player starts 100% mortal with no HUD or powers until First Death (<= 12% HP or >= 85% Knox infection) awakens Tier 1. Survive Temporary Death, seek sanctuary on Holy Ground or at Duncan MacLeod’s Louisville Antique Store (12640, 1840), trade Watcher journals, and defeat 6 villains before facing Jacob Kell for The Prize.

Divine Cheats & Vehicle Suite

All-in-one vehicle workshop and sandbox toolkit built natively for Build 41 and Build 42 without needing -debug launch flags.

Right-click any car for 100% repair, refueling, hotwiring, ignition keys, vehicle god mode, or a paginated spawner covering Vanilla, Filibuster Rhymes, Autotsar (ATA), and KI5 packs. Right-click anywhere for God Mode, 10,000 carry weight, ghost mode, 1-tick actions, death aura, max skills, all recipes, 5 supply kits, and town quick travel.

An American in Camelot

Locks all NPC factions, pawn gear, and non-Empire world loot to the Medieval era while your own colony researches freely up to Ultra and Archotech.

The Shattered Empire (Royalty) is limited to a single neutral outpost that trades and sends Royal Tribute Collectors exclusively to you. Runtime Harmony patching leaves XML Defs intact alongside Vanilla, World Tech Level, Medieval Overhaul, and VFE Medieval 2.
