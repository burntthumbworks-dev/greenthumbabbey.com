About the Workshop

Mods, weird sandboxes, and fun side projects.

Greenthumb Abbey is the community modding and creative sandbox workshop of Burnt Thumb Works, released under the banner of His Grace The Duke of Errol, The Dude of Greenthumb Abbey.

Nothing here is behind a paywall. It’s where lore-faithful overhauls, debug/vehicle toolkits, and game-breaking “what if?” experiments live alongside guides, controls, and Steam Workshop links.

Full commercial games and studio apps live over at burntthumbworks.com.

How mods here are built

Honest status labels

If a mod is Alpha WIP, it says Alpha WIP. Features, hotkeys, and version support (like Project Zomboid B41 & B42 or RimWorld 1.5–1.7) are listed straight up.

Non-destructive compatibility

Dynamic vehicle pack scanning in Zomboid and runtime Harmony patching in RimWorld instead of overwriting vanilla files.

Zero-bloat static site

Pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS—no trackers, no login walls, fast on desktop and mobile.
